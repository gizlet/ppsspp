PPSSPP 1.20.4, the PSP emulator, built from its upstream source for Gizlet handhelds such as the R36S.

The package contains no games, BIOS files or game artwork. PPSSPP opens its game browser on your TF2 card when one is mounted, or in the folder Emulation Center chose; open the PSP games you own from there.

Saves, save states and settings are kept on PPSSPP's own memory stick in the app's save folder, so your card is only read. PPSSPP's own update check is turned off: updates come through the Gizlet Store.

**Controls.** The D-pad, sticks and face buttons work as on a PSP; A is Circle and B is Cross. L2 opens PPSSPP's menu during a game. Holding FN, or Select and Start for two seconds, returns to the Gizlet launcher.
